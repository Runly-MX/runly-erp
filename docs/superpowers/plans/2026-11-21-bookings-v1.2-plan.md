# Reservaciones v1.2 plan (R1-R3)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-bookings-v1.2-resources-meetings-calendars-design.md`. Each delivery starts
only when the user asks; R1 is the current one.

| Delivery | Scope | Closes when |
|---|---|---|
| R1 Resources | Migration (`purpose`, internal service, `title`, `organizer_id`, `booking_participant`), resource-space creation, room routes/service on the engine, projection into organizer/participant calendars, "Reservar sala" screen, Calendar entry point, `bookings.reserve`. | PostgreSQL: 20 employees booking one room at once → 1; capacity, hours, max length, permissions (`reservers`), organizer/agent cancel and move, cross-company isolation, idempotent retry. | R2 Meetings | Audit of calls; service `meeting` setting; `booking_meeting` adapter (prepare/revoke/reschedule, idempotent, sweep); join from email and manage page with guest lobby. | E2E: external guest joins through lobby; reschedule keeps one room; cancel revokes; no orphan rooms; cross-company links refused. |
| R3 Calendars | Google/Outlook links, signed `.ics` (UID, SEQUENCE, VTIMEZONE), update/cancel `.ics` in emails, docs of limits. | `.ics` validated by parser tests (DST, IANA zone); token never in calendar URLs; email E2E. |

## R1 tasks

1. Migration + Prisma models; validators (`purpose`, policy `reservers`/`maxMinutes`, reservation schema); SDK.
2. Config: resource-space creation creates the internal service; space resources sync its links; internal service
   hidden from `listServices`.
3. `bookings-rooms-service.js` + routes: list, agenda, create/cancel/reschedule via `createTx`/`transitionTx`/
   `rescheduleTx` with participants in `sideEffect`; access rules.
4. Calendar projection: per-person default calendars for resource reservations.
5. UI: "Reservar sala" screen + reservation dialog + "Mis reservaciones"; space creation choice and trimmed tabs;
   Calendar "Reservar sala" action.
6. Tests: PostgreSQL suite; `scripts/verify-bookings-rooms.mjs` (disposable stack, browser).

## R1 status (2026-11-21): closed

- Migration `20270111000000_bookings_resource_reservations`: `booking_space.purpose`, `booking_service.internal` (one
  per space, partial unique index), `booking.title`/`organizer_id`, `booking_participant` (RLS, client roles revoked).
- A "Salas y recursos" system is created with its internal service and office hours (Mon-Fri 08:00-20:00, editable in
  Disponibilidad); linking resources keeps the internal service links in step; services and portal pages are refused
  (`RESOURCE_SPACE`) and the internal service never appears in lists or editors.
- `bookings-rooms-service.js` + `bookings-rooms-routes.js` (`/bookings/rooms/*`, `bookings.reserve` or
  `bookings.access`): reservations are engine bookings with the resource pinned, length = end - start (15-minute grid,
  up to `policy.maxMinutes`), party = max(headcount, 1 + participants); title, organizer and participants written in the
  engine transaction. Policy `reservers` (`company` default | `members`). Cancel/move by the organizer or space
  agents/admins; anyone else gets 404. Agenda shows busy blocks from every space sharing the resource; motive only to
  people involved and space managers.
- Calendar projection: room reservations also land in the default calendar of the organizer and each participant
  (created like Calendar does when missing), reconciled per calendar; cancelling removes them all.
- UI: "Reservar sala" (`/rooms`, nav entry), day grid per resource (phone: one room at a time), reservation dialog
  (room, motive, start, end, participants, headcount), "Mis reservaciones" with move/cancel; new-system choice
  "Servicios y citas | Salas y recursos"; resource systems hide Servicios and Reservas online and add "Quién puede
  reservar" and "Duración máxima" to Configuración; Calendar toolbar "Reservar sala" opens the same dialog (shown only
  to people who may reserve some room). Manifest 0.3.0 with `bookings.reserve` (module sync after deploy).
- Verification: `node scripts/verify-bookings-rooms.mjs` (disposable stack, real auth, Chromium): 20/20 on
  2026-11-21: bookings PostgreSQL suites 48/48 (rooms 8/8: 20 employees on one room → 1, overlap refused and touching
  ranges allowed, capacity/hours/length/grid, `reservers`, organizer vs manager cancel/move, idempotent replay and
  reused key 422, shared room across spaces, cross-company 404, calendars of organizer and participants incl. one
  created on demand), ticketing suites 28/28; over HTTP two employees with 10 simultaneous requests → one 201;
  browser: rooms system created from the UI, phone (dark) reservation with participant, desktop conflict message
  from a stale screen, participant sees it in "Mis reservaciones" and in Calendar, reserve from Calendar, no button
  without permission, no overflow, no console errors. Calendar suites (123 tests) also green against the same kind of disposable database.
- Limits: people with only `bookings.reserve` (without `bookings.access`) reserve from Calendar, because module
  visibility needs `bookings.access`. Recurring and multi-resource reservations remain non-goals.
